# Classic data structures — alternating Q/A lines
What is the time complexity of binary search?
O(log n) on a sorted collection

Q: Difference between a stack and a queue?
A: Stack is LIFO (push/pop), queue is FIFO (enqueue/dequeue).

What is a hash map collision?
Two distinct keys hash to the same bucket, resolved by chaining or open addressing

Q: When would you use a linked list over an array?
A: When you need O(1) insert/delete at known positions and don't need random access

What is an AVL tree?
A self-balancing BST where heights of child subtrees differ by at most one at every node
